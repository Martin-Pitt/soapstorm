# Region Object Cache (ROC): instant-region ghost cache + aux data, FullID-keyed sidecar over LLVOCache

Status: DESIGNED 2026-08-27, awaiting owner sign-off. Produced from an 8-agent design workflow (3 subsystem readers — LSL bridge, object pipeline, region aux data; 2 competing designs under opposed priors; adversarial critique of each; adjudication). Line-level claims verified against the tree; the winning design's critique confirmed nearly every citation exactly. Companion to `doc/super_compressed_textures.md` (the BC7 tier) — the ROC feeds the BC7 region manifests and shares its container discipline.

## Verdicts (TLDR)

1. **Rez time is NOT obtainable from the viewer protocol — the hoped-for direct query does not exist.** message_template.msg has no RezTime token anywhere; ObjectProperties carries only CreationDate (U64 usec — the prim/asset CREATION date, which survives take-and-re-rez and says nothing about time standing in the region); ObjectPropertiesFamily has no date field at all. Proven distinct inside this fork: LSL exposes OBJECT_CREATION_TIME and OBJECT_REZ_TIME as separate values and the FS bridge already round-trips both into different fields (fs_resources script line 611; fslslbridge.cpp:487). Persistence verification is therefore DUAL-PATH: bridge `RocSweep r` batched llGetObjectDetails([OBJECT_REZ_TIME]) as accelerator, cross-visit FullID/position stability as the mandatory bridge-free fallback (sole path on OpenSim, where bridge creation is compiled out). Never use protocol CreationDate as a persistence signal.
2. **Store: a NEW FullID-keyed sidecar** `<cache>/roc/<grid>/<region_handle>.roc`, layered OVER the untouched LLVOCache/.slc. The losing design extended .slc in place and died on a structural fact: .slc is CacheID-gated and a sim restart (roughly weekly on SL) discards the whole file (llvocache.cpp:1592-1596), so promotion counters kept there rarely survive long enough to promote anything. The sidecar is restart-immune, needs no INDRA_OBJECT_CACHE_VERSION bump (no global user cache wipe), deletes per-file on corruption, and iterates freely.
3. **Ghost lifecycle: TTL derezzes visuals only; records die only on explicit refutation.** Silence is never evidence — an unconfirmed ghost disappearing at TTL is usually interest-list neglect, not deletion. A guard-passing bridge answered-missing is the one absolute refutation; an unsolicited sim KillObject is absolute for derez but quarantines rather than evicts a tenured record; a kill provoked by our own select probe is half weight and never evicts (KillObject carries no reason field, so it cannot distinguish deletion from an interest-list cull). Accumulated refutations trash the whole region cache (the owner's 1-2 minute rule).
4. **Aux data ships FIRST and alone** (Phase 1): heightmap, water height and terrain composition at frame one of a revisit — decoupled from any object work and from the 600s stay gate (the crossing/sailing traveler on a 2048m-draw-distance fork is a prime beneficiary; aux saves on every exit).
5. **Bridge protocol: one new command** (`RocSweep`, script v2.33→2.34 with the established auto-update dance), HTTP-reply-only, ≤50 dashed keys/request (the radar's 60-key "precedent" is a latent overflow of the 2048-byte LSL http_request cap — fix its 61-key loop in the same pass, fsradar.cpp:667). Replies carry the checked count, the present count and the missing keys in full; six acceptance guards mean no reply shape — including no reply at all — can manufacture a refutation. Contract in `doc/roc_bridge_spec.md`.
6. **Manifest feed: one-way ROC→BC7.** At region-exit save the ROC walks promoted objects' DP blobs (TextureEntry + sculpt/mesh UUIDs) + terrain detail UUIDs and regenerates the BC7 region manifest — geometry from ROC, pixels from BC7, same LLWorld::addRegion trigger on revisit.
7. **Auto-return immunity is a hard promotion gate, not a score term** — and the About Land "Show objects" batch mechanism cannot serve as its mechanism (permission-gated, returns local IDs only, clobbers the user's selection). It survives as an opt-in accelerator on your own land. The working ladder is: owner UUID free from the cached blob → one ParcelProperties request per parcel (for owner, group and the auto-return timer) → a select probe for the group-set residue, or one `RocSweep a` where the bridge exists. When none of that settles it, the object earns immunity **empirically** by being confirmed on several distinct days — auto-return timers are minutes, so survival across days is proof in itself. Conversely, once immunity IS established it substitutes for waiting: proof that an object cannot be swept away is exactly what the day count was standing in for, so promotion switches to counting visits (dwell included) instead.

## What exists today (verified by the readers)

- VOCache: objects_X_Y.slc loaded SYNCHRONOUSLY at handshake (llviewerregion.cpp:3427); entries untrusted until the sim's ObjectUpdateCached probe flood CRC-validates them — and probes are interest-list-driven, frustum + distance gated, so behind-camera objects wait for a camera turn (the fork's own comment complains at llviewerregion.cpp:1823-1826). local_id keying dies on sim restart; 128-slot LRU; version bump wipes every user's whole object cache.
- The rez path from cached DP blobs exists end-to-end: createVisibleObjects → processObjectUpdateFromCache (llviewerobjectlist.cpp:395-502), FullID drawable adoption via LLVOCacheEntry::setOctreeEntry (llvocache.cpp:311-326), local-id rebind blocks (llviewerobjectlist.cpp:438-456, 675-705). The ROC reuses all of it unmodified.
- Progress-screen unthrottle: sNewObjectCreationThrottle=-2 while the TP screen shows (llviewerregion.cpp:1795-1798) — but the createVisibleObjects break condition never fires at -2 (1612), so injection needs its own per-frame ceiling.
- killInvisibleObjects reaps up to 64 ACTIVE objects/frame outside the rear radius (llviewerregion.cpp:1820-1904) seconds after the screen lifts — any ghost design that hangs state on the LLViewerObject alone loses it here.
- FS LSL Bridge: invisible attachment prim + viewer-uploaded script; viewer→script via HTTP POST to a per-region llRequestSecureURL, script→viewer via llHTTPResponse (synchronous, per-request-correlated — use this) or tagged llOwnerSay (async, uncorrelated, ~1023B chat cap — avoid). URL dies on region change and mCurrentURL is NOT cleared (sends fail silently, log-only); bridge-ready arrives SECONDS after entry; absent on OpenSim (#if compiled out), when UseLSLBridge is off, or script-mute estates. The script already calls llGetObjectDetails on arbitrary keys (getZOffsets sweeps; getScriptInfo already returns OBJECT_REZ_TIME at script:611) — RocSweep is a straightforward else-if in the dispatcher.
- ObjectProperties requires an ObjectSelect and is not answered for attachments; the fork already has a silent select→probe precedent (llselectmgr.cpp:6165-6199) and the select→KillObject auto-cull principle (llviewerobject.cpp:420-427; fires on edit/right-click select, NOT plain left-click).

## Store format

`roc/<grid>/<region_handle>.roc` — handle is the only pre-handshake key; RegionID stored inside for validation. Header: magic 'SROC', version, region_handle, region_id, last_cache_id, saved_at, sessions_seen, flags, section table (offset/size/crc32 each), file CRC32. Sections:

- **[OBJECTS]** root-before-children: {FullID, parent FullID, last_local_id, crc, update_flags (captured LIVE at message time — verified absent from the .slc disk format, llvocache.cpp:435-454), score, first_seen, last_confirmed, sessions_seen, crc_change_count, rez_time_epoch (0=unknown), **child_count captured from the live object at save** (root blobs carry no child enumeration — without this, linkset completeness is undecidable and floating fragments ship), pos, scale, DP blob ≤10000B}. The DP blob is byte-identical to the .slc blob so every existing static unpacker and the whole OUT_FULL_CACHED rez path reuse unmodified.
- **[GLTF]** material overrides keyed by ObjectId FullID (fixes the stock sidecar's local_id-keyed-therefore-restart-worthless GLTF cache).
- **[AUX]** see below. **[MANIFEST]** deduped asset-UUID export for the BC7 tier.

Caps: soft 4MB / hard 32MB per region (score-ranked truncation); 2GB global watermark for roc/ with lax last-visit eviction, mirroring the BC7 policy. Corrupt CRC → delete that file only. LOAD is async on a worker (kicked at LLWorld::addRegion, llworld.cpp:520), delivering a ready-to-inject vector to the main loop — unlike the stock synchronous .slc read. SAVE: main thread snapshots DP buffers + counters at region destruct (≤32MB worst case, typically <4MB), worker serializes and writes whole-file. SHUTDOWN: worker saves must drain behind a flush barrier before LLVOCache/thread-pool teardown (the LLVOCache dtor writes its header, llvocache.cpp:1195-1203) — designed in Phase 0.

## Ghost state machine (settled)

States live on the ROC record / cache entry, NEVER only on the LLViewerObject: RECORDED → GHOST_PENDING → GHOST_LIVE ⇄ GHOST_PARKED (object reaped by killInvisibleObjects or other non-ROC kill; record keeps ghost status, TTL keeps ticking) → CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED_DIRTY | EXPIRED (derezzed, record KEPT, score×0.5) | REFUTED (record evicted) | TRASHED (region file deleted).

1. **Ghost bit placement** (the one HIGH flaw both critiques shared, fixed): consulted at EVERY rez path — initial injection AND re-rez after invisibility culling — so a behind-camera ghost reaped at T+5s re-rezzes on camera turn STILL suppressed.
2. **TTL semantics**: expiry (120s mode A / 90s mode B) derezzes the VISUAL only, marks EXPIRED, score×0.5, NEUTRAL to the error budget. Eviction only on explicit refutation.
3. **Suppression matrix while ghost**: attached sound (llviewerobject.cpp:2119), particles (2062), hover text (2013-2048), omega (1976-1980), media/MOAP via checkMediaURL (2050-2055); flags stay 0 (no touch/pay/sit affordances). Derender/blacklist filters retained. One helper predicate, one test matrix. Tolerate probe-sets-flags-before-rez ordering.
4. **Confirm rules** (any one): (a) ObjectUpdateCached probe CRC-match → full confirm + loadFlags via the ACTIVE branch (llviewerregion.cpp:3083-3106); (b) any full/compressed update with matching FullID within the neighbourhood — |dPos| ≤ max(1.0m, 25% bounding radius), scale within 5% → confirm; CRC changed or pos beyond → CONFIRMED_DIRTY (write-back at exit); (c) bridge sweep 'present' → soft-confirm (render kept, TTL cleared, flags stay 0 until a real packet).
5. **local_id-reuse rule** (mode A): a CRC-mismatch probe or an update carrying a DIFFERENT FullID for an injected entry's local_id immediately derezzes that ghost and releases the id — neutral, not refutation (the id was recycled; the ghost's fate is decided by FullID reconciliation or TTL). Without this, a reused id shows the wrong object for two minutes.
6. **Injection**: after RegionHandshakeReply is composed (bit0/bit1 NEVER altered — the sim-facing contract is untouched, llviewerregion.cpp:3439-3450); idempotent (skip if local_id occupied in mCacheMap or the FullID is already live — live always wins); PER-FRAME CEILING ~200 even during the throttle=-2 window; hard per-region cap ~2000 score-ranked. V1: ghosts for the AGENT'S region only; neighbors get aux-only (the bridge can't verify neighbors and their interest lists are distance-gated — neighbor ghosts would mass-expire).
7. **.slc isolation**: injected entries carry a ROC-injected flag; at saveObjectCache, unconfirmed injected entries are excluded from writeToCache (llviewerregion.cpp:877 writes the whole map otherwise — verified leak). One additional SS:Nexii-tagged stock touch, honestly accounted.
8. **Mode B (CacheID mismatch / sim restart)**: no mCacheMap injection, no local-id registration — the skip flag goes in createObjectFromCache next to setUUIDAndLocal (llviewerobjectlist.cpp:2250; keep the mUUIDObjectMap insert so FullID adoption works); mbCanSelect=false; FullID-only reconciliation; bridge is the primary culler; TTL 90s.
9. **Trash-all**: refutation counter R ≥ max(10, 20% of ghosts spawned) within the first 120s, OR a guard-passing bridge sweep reporting ≥20% of CHECKED keys missing → kill remaining ghosts frame-budgeted over ≤2s, delete the region .roc, purge injected entries from mCacheMap, rebuild fresh at exit. R counts only *unsolicited* resolved KillObjects and answered-missing keys — never a kill we provoked with our own probe, and never silence — each weighted by (1 − ½·shield) so a region full of well-tenured records resists a false trash. Border objects within 10m of a region edge weigh 0.5 and need two hits (llGetObjectDetails [] also means 'crossed out'). A killed ghost's UUID never blocks legitimate later recreation (IGNORE_DEAD off).

## Promotion pipeline (settled 2026-08-27, step by step)

Five stages. Every hook below was verified against the tree. Objects are promoted only at region-exit save, and only after clearing three independent hurdles: immunity, persistence, and score.

**Stage A — sighting (visit 1, zero added traffic).** The `.roc` ledger loads async at `LLWorld::addRegion`. One hook, one line after `entry->setUpdateFlags(flags)` at `llviewerregion.cpp:2977` — the common tail of all three `cacheFullUpdate` branches — covers first sighting, dupe-confirm and mutation without double counting. A second hook in `probeCache`'s CRC-match branch (`llviewerregion.cpp:3081`) creates records for warm-`.slc` revisits, where the probe flood is the *only* thing that ever mentions most objects. All object data is read offline from the byte-identical blob via the existing side-effect-free unpackers: FullID@0, LocalID@16, PCode@20, State@21, CRC@22, Scale@28, Pos@40, SpecialCode@64, **Owner@68**. `update_flags` must be captured live here — they are absent from the `.slc` disk layout, and a cache-rezzed ghost reports every flag as *false* rather than *unknown*, so immunity logic must never read flags off a cache-rezzed object. Hard disqualifiers (physics, character, avatar PCode, State≠0, oversized blob) mark the record and it is never re-evaluated. Before any of that, a region carrying `REGION_FLAGS_SANDBOX` short-circuits object recording entirely — see the sandbox rules under auto-return immunity.

**Stage B — parcel classification (visit 1, T+20s, ≤64 requests).** The parcel overlay gives region-wide boundaries for free (1 byte per 4m cell, west/south line flags, both accessors public). Union-find over those flags enumerates the region's parcels; one `ParcelPropertiesRequest` per parcel under a new `SSROC_PARCEL_SEQ_ID = -60000` harvests OwnerID, IsGroupOwned, GroupID, **CleanOtherTime**, prim buckets and the membership bitmap. Two `<SS:Nexii>` touches in `llviewerparcelmgr.cpp`: a new sequence-id branch before the catch-all at :1690 (today −60000 is silently dropped), and a hard return right after `parcel->unpackMessage(msg)` at :1815 — *before* the agent-parcel bitmap, `changeParcels()`, media autoplay and observer notification. No selection, no yellow highlight, no dwell or access-list request, nothing user-visible. A `PARCEL_RESULT_MULTIPLE` reply means the segmentation was wrong and aborts the stage for that visit. Child prims must be resolved through their root: the compressed unpack reads position as *parent-relative* for `ParentID != 0`, so filing children by raw position would put the whole region in cell (0,0).

**Stage C — active classification of the residue (visit 1, T+45s onward).** Only records still unresolved on a parcel with auto-return armed and non-immune prims present. A new batched overload of `requestObjectPropertiesViaSelect` (body lifted from the proven FSAreaSearch loop) yields three things per round trip: affirmative existence confirmation that the current code discards, plus OwnerID **and GroupID** — the only client-side source of an object's group. Cadence: not before T+15s, 64 ids/batch, one batch per region per 2s, ≤200 outstanding, ≥30s per-record retry, max 2 probes per record per session, with a blanket deselect flush every 60s and at region exit. Where the LSL bridge is available, a single `RocSweep a` replaces this entire stage.

**Stage D — promotion decision (region-exit, worker thread).** Scoring runs only after a settled stay (600s, the same condition the stock save already uses); the ledger itself updates on every visit regardless. Three hurdles in order:
1. **Immunity** — a hard gate, evaluated before score and never a score term. A penalty would be fungible and could be bought back by unrelated terms, which is exactly wrong for a safety gate.
2. **Persistence** — satisfied by ANY of: bridge rez-age ≥48h; established auto-return immunity plus `SSROCPromoteVisits` visits (visits include dwell, see the tenure section); or, with no immunity established, `SSROCPromoteDaysUnproven` distinct calendar days. Never score alone, never protocol CreationDate.
3. **Score** ≥ `SSROCPromoteScore` (default 0.40 — deliberately permissive; the immunity gate above is what keeps the cache honest, so the score only has to filter out obviously dynamic content), weights below.

Every non-promoted record stores a one-byte `blocked_by` (SCORE / IMMUNITY / PERSISTENCE / STAY / DISQUALIFIED) and the debug HUD reports the histogram — without it a three-hurdle gate is undebuggable in the field.

**Stage E — next visit.** Only promoted records inject, ordered by `score × shield × (1 − 0.1·min(miss_streak,5))`: doubt is expressed as paint order, never as deletion. Then the T+2s existence sweep, TTL derez of visuals only, and rescore at exit.

**How long until anything ghosts, with the shipped defaults.** Where auto-return immunity is established — your own land, your group's land, any parcel with auto-return off, or anything the bridge confirms — promotion needs 2 visits, and two hours of presence counts as the second. So **a region you actually spend an evening in ghosts on your next arrival**, same day. Where immunity could not be established, the conservative path applies: 3 distinct calendar days. With the bridge present and rez-age confirmed, promotion happens at the end of visit 1 regardless. Until an object is promoted the object side behaves exactly as today — same probe-driven pop-in, no regression, and Stages B and C are invisible to the user.

## Auto-return immunity (settled)

**What is actually proven client-side is exactly one thing: that a given FullID was still present in this region on N distinct days.** Every structural leg below is strong evidence resting on something this repository cannot verify, and is labelled as such rather than being allowed to imply certainty.

- **Tier 0 — free, offline, zero traffic.** The object's Owner UUID sits at blob offset 68 in every cached object. Unverified dependency: whether the sim populates it for objects you do not own (the tree argues both ways).
- **Tier 1 — one request per parcel.** Authoritative when populated. The dangerous failure direction: if the sim withholds `CleanOtherTime` from agents with no rights on a parcel, a withheld zero is indistinguishable from a real zero and would produce *false* immunity — so "auto-return disarmed" alone never immunises, it needs corroborating confirmed days.
- **Tier 2 — the two group cases, which are NOT the same thing.** Auto-return spares objects *set to the parcel's group* as well as those owned by the parcel owner: the About Land Objects tab's three buckets are exactly "owned by parcel owner", "set to group" and "owned by others", and only the last is swept. So the group test is against `LLParcel::getGroupID()` — the group the parcel is set to, read straight from ParcelProperties into `mGroupID` (llparcel.cpp:586) — and it applies whether the parcel is resident-owned-with-a-group or deeded to that group.
  - **Deeded objects are free.** `FLAGS_OBJECT_GROUP_OWNED` (object_flags.h:51) means the object was deeded to a group, so its OwnerID field *is* the group UUID — and OwnerID is already in the cached blob at offset 68. A deeded object whose owner matches the parcel's group is therefore settled offline, no probe at all.
  - **Set-to-group objects need the probe.** These keep a resident owner and carry a *separate* GroupID, which appears in no object update and has no flag anywhere in `object_flags.h`. This is the only case costing a select probe or a `RocSweep a` — and on group-built land it is the common one, which is what makes that tier worth having.
- **Tier 3 — the About Land "Show" mechanism: REJECTED as the mechanism, kept as an opt-in accelerator, default off.** `ForceObjectSelect` carries local IDs and nothing else — no FullID, no owner, no group, and no indication of *which* bucket matched. It is permission-gated to agents holding land-return powers on that parcel, its stock handler destroys the user's current selection and paints silhouettes, and it is one of six callers sharing an identical reply so a naive harvest could mistake an RT_OTHER click for an ownership answer. Useful only on your own or your group's land.

**Auto-return is a PER-PARCEL setting**, so immunity is always decided per parcel, against the owner, group and `CleanOtherTime` of the specific parcel an object stands on — never per region. Two sandbox cases are decided up front (owner, 2026-08-27):

- **Region carries `REGION_FLAGS_SANDBOX` → never record objects there at all.** Only Linden Lab can set that flag, so it is authoritative rather than a guess, and sandbox content is transient by definition — an auto-cleaned build platform is the worst possible thing to cache and rez back as scenery. Flags arrive in `unpackRegionHandshake` (llviewerregion.cpp:3276) and can change mid-session via RegionInfo (llviewermessage.cpp:5380), so this is re-checked rather than latched. Aux data (terrain relief, water level, ground textures) is still cached for these regions — terrain is not affected by object return and the win is real for anyone who sandboxes regularly. Say if you would rather skip them entirely.
- **Parcel whose name contains "sandbox" → no immunity by default.** This is a heuristic, not a fact, since anyone can name a parcel anything — so it does *not* skip recording. It denies the structural immunity credit, dropping those objects onto the conservative distinct-days path where they must survive real days before being cached. A false positive costs patience, never correctness; with `SSROCRequireAutoReturnProof` enabled they are simply never cached. Case-insensitive substring, so "LR Sandbox 3", "Sandbox Island" and "build-sandbox-2" all match.

**Null safety is part of the gate, not a detail.** `LLParcel::isPublic()` is literally `mOwnerID.isNull()`, and group IDs are nulled on ungrouped parcels — so without a non-null guard on both sides, `null == null` would immunise every object on Linden and abandoned land.

**When immunity cannot be established, the object is not promoted with a penalty — it stays provisional and keeps accumulating days**, becoming immune empirically at 3 distinct days spanning 2 (or 5 days spanning 7 if we classified its parcel as armed — if a FullID survives that long on land we called dangerous, our classification was wrong and observation beats classification). This is sound because auto-return timers are measured in *minutes*, and because records are FullID-keyed a re-rezzed replacement is a different record, so a re-rezzing vendor cannot fake tenure. Not covered by any tier, and stated plainly: prim-limit overage returns (which take the parcel owner's *own* objects, defeating the owner-match leg), estate-manager mass returns, and returns triggered by land sale. Only repeated observation catches those.

## Promotion scoring (settled)

Computed on linkset ROOTS at region-exit save, on the worker from a main-thread snapshot; children ride the root. Signals: (a) .slc counters READ-only at save — mHitCount/mDupeCount/mCRCChangeCount; (b) update_flags captured live at message time; (c) DP-blob features via existing static unpackers (PCode, SpecialCode bits, scale, ParentID); (d) ROC cross-session history — first_seen, last_confirmed, sessions_seen, per-session position delta (restart-immune because FullID-keyed); (e) bridge rez_age.

HARD DISQUALIFIERS: FLAGS_USE_PHYSICS, avatar PCode, attachment State ≠ 0, KillObject observed this session, blob > 10000B. SCORE (0..1): 0.40 persistence (bridge rez_age min(days,30)/30, else cross-visit span × 0.75 discount; renormalize when a signal class is structurally unavailable) + 0.25 familiarity (min(sessions_seen,5)/5, ALSO creditable by bridge existence-confirmation — this fixes small-object starvation, where interest-list-omitted objects never earn probe counters; the owner's motivating class must be promotable) + 0.20 stability (CRC churn + position drift within neighbourhood) + 0.15 confirmation (min(hits+dupes,20)/20). BONUS: **+`SSROCPublicWorksBonus` (default 0.25) for objects owned by a Mole** — the Linden Department of Public Works builds the permanent landscape of the mainland (roads, bridges, terraforming, public builds), placed deliberately by the estate owner's own department, so it is the most cacheable content on the grid. Mole ownership also grants the auto-return immunity credit outright: LDPW content stands on Linden land by arrangement, so the owner-match test — which compares against the *parcel* owner, usually Governor Linden rather than the Mole — would otherwise miss it entirely. Moving public works (trains and the like) need no carve-out: they are physical, and `FLAGS_USE_PHYSICS` is a hard disqualifier evaluated **before** any score, so no bonus can resurrect one. Detection is `ssROCOwnerIsPublicWorks()` on the resolved legacy name of the blob's owner UUID, cached into `SSROC_REC_OWNER_PUBLIC_WORKS` so the name lookup happens once per object.

PENALTIES: scripted −0.10, touch-handler −0.05, particles/omega/sound −0.05; phantom none. PROMOTE iff score ≥ 0.50 AND persistence evidence exists: bridge rez_age ≥ 48h OR same-FullID-within-neighbourhood in ≥2 sessions ≥24h apart — never score alone, never CreationDate. DEMOTION: CRC change halves stored score; position beyond neighbourhood → 0.25 probation (landscaping re-verifies, not evicted); EXPIRED → ×0.5; REFUTED → evicted; trash-all → file deleted.

## Two tiers: recent and long-term (owner amendment 2026-08-27)

Promotion answers "is this object permanent enough to remember for weeks". That is the wrong question for the commonest case in actual use — hopping between a handful of regions in one sitting. **A region's contents do not meaningfully change over a few hours**, so an object confirmed present twenty minutes ago is overwhelmingly likely to still be there, whatever its long-term standing.

So there are two independent reasons to draw a cached object on arrival:

- **RECENT** — it was confirmed within `SSROCRecentGhostMinutes` (default 180). No score, no immunity gate, no day counting. The evidence is recency, and it is strong evidence over that horizon.
- **PROMOTED** — it cleared the immunity, persistence and score gates, and is worth drawing after days or weeks away.

**This needs no new storage.** The `.roc` file already holds every record up to the region cap rather than only promoted ones, and every record carries `mLastConfirmed`, so the recency population is already on disk and already loaded on arrival. The tier is one predicate at injection time: `isPromoted() || (now - mLastConfirmed) < window`. That also makes it survive a viewer restart, which an in-memory session cache would not.

The hard disqualifiers still apply — physical objects, avatars, attachments and oversized blobs are never drawn from cache on any tier. Auto-return immunity deliberately does NOT apply to the recent tier: an auto-return timer is measured in minutes, so an object could in principle be swept between visits, but the ghost machinery already covers exactly this case — an unconfirmed ghost derezzes at TTL and an answered-missing sweep evicts it. Waiting days to protect against a case the reconciliation layer already handles would give up the feature's best moment for nothing.

**Why this matters for development order:** the recent tier is what makes the ghost pipeline testable now. Promotion cannot produce a ghost until day three at the earliest, so validating ghost rendering, confirmation and refutation against promoted records means waiting days between iterations. The recent tier produces thousands of ghost candidates on the second visit to a region in the same session, which is the fast feedback loop the reconciliation logic needs. Build ghosts against the recent tier first; promotion then feeds the same machinery with no further work.

The exit diagnostic reports a `recent` count alongside `promoted`, so the size of this population is measurable before any ghost rendering exists.

## Tenure and demotion resistance (settled)

**Two currencies, chosen by whether auto-return immunity was established (owner amendment 2026-08-27).**

- **Immunity established → count VISITS.** Auto-return proof already answers the question days were being used as a proxy for: an object owned by the parcel owner or set to the parcel group cannot be swept away on a timer, so waiting several calendar days to confirm what is structurally guaranteed is wasted patience. A visit is one region entry, plus one more for every `SSROCVisitHours` (default 2h) of confirmed presence — an evening in your home region is worth several drive-bys past a stranger's build, which is the honest reading of the evidence. Default `SSROCPromoteVisits` is 2, so a couple of hours somewhere you actually spend time is enough.
- **Immunity NOT established → count DISTINCT CALENDAR DAYS**, as before, default 3 (`SSROCPromoteDaysUnproven`). Here dwell buys nothing: an object on an auto-return timer can vanish between one hour and the next, and only the passage of real days is evidence that it did not. `SSROCRequireAutoReturnProof` (default off) turns this path off entirely for the strictest posture.

For the conservative path the currency remains **distinct calendar days on which the object was affirmatively confirmed**, not sessions and not elapsed time: two teleports in one evening are two sessions but one day of evidence. Each record carries a 64-bit day bitmap, `first_seen_day`, `last_seen_day`, and a monotonic `confirm_days`. Derived: `tenure_days` = span, `recent7` = popcount of the low 7 bits. Both span and distinct-days are needed — a three-year-old build visited monthly has huge span and few days, and that is correct.

**A two-sided clock guard is mandatory.** A single forward jump (VM resume, bad NTP, dual-boot RTC) writes a future `last_seen_day`; when the clock corrects, every later confirmation takes the backward branch and sets bit 0 of a stale future day forever, freezing the day count. So: forward jumps beyond a decade are ignored, backward slips of one day are absorbed, and larger backward jumps re-anchor the bitmap while preserving `first_seen_day`.

**What silence does: nothing.** A visit with no confirmation increments `miss_streak` and that is its complete effect — it does not touch the day bitmap, `confirm_days`, score, immunity or promotion state. `miss_streak` feeds exactly two things: injection paint order, and verification priority (escalating a record to the head of the next sweep). This is the direct answer to "the interest list simply is not showing it".

**Protection tiers** scale with earned tenure — ESTABLISHED (2 days), TENURED (3 days spanning 6), LANDMARK (6 days spanning 20) — and raise the soft-refutation threshold accordingly, with LANDMARK effectively immune to soft evidence.

**Refutation credits, and what is absolute:**

| Evidence | Weight |
|---|---|
| Bridge sweep answered-missing, all six guards passed | **ABSOLUTE** — evicts at every tier |
| Unsolicited KillObject resolving to this record's FullID | absolute for derez; evicts below TENURED, quarantines at or above |
| Unsolicited KillObject, unresolved local id | strong, not absolute — the sim sends an identical message for an interest-list cull |
| KillObject in reply to our own select probe | 0.5, never evicts at any tier |
| Ghost TTL expiry, silence, no reply, any truncation | 0.0 |
| Border object (within 10m of a region edge) | ×0.5 on all of the above |

This **downgrades the earlier verdict that select-probe kills evict records**. `KillObject` carries an object id and nothing else — no reason, no deleted-versus-culled distinction — so a kill we provoked is weaker evidence than one the sim volunteered. Soft refutation decays once per confirmed day, not per confirmation, or the probe flood at the start of every visit would annihilate it before any threshold could be reached.

**Quarantine** replaces eviction for tenured records: stop ghosting, keep the record and every accumulated day, and force it to the head of a targeted sweep on the next three visits. Three answered-missing evicts; any affirmative confirmation restores it. **The bridge-unavailable exit closes the immortal-ghost hole**: on OpenSim or with the bridge off, quarantine's only exit does not exist and a demolished build generates no kills either, so after five consecutive cold visits a record stops injecting while keeping all its evidence.

**The complete set of things that can remove a record:** an answered-missing sweep; a resolved unsolicited kill (below TENURED); score-rank truncation at the region byte cap — a *capacity* decision, not an existence claim, and the only bridge-free removal path, with a quarter of the cap reserved for the highest-tenure candidates so a dense region doesn't truncate away its aging candidates before they can mature; trash-all; and newly tripping a hard disqualifier. Parcel fingerprint mismatch never removes — it drops immunity and puts a promoted record on probation.

**Decoupling rule — the single most important correctness invariant.** No ROC code may key eviction off any stock erosion path. The `.slc` has eight of them, including the `isValid()` write filter that drops unconfirmed entries after a settled stay and `updateCacheEntry`'s kill of a nudged object's entry. A chair that someone nudges loses its `.slc` entry; it must not lose its ROC record.

## Bridge protocol (v2.34)

Full wire contract, guards and hand-off notes live in `doc/roc_bridge_spec.md`. Summary: one new dispatcher branch, `RocSweep`, with modes `e` (existence), `r` (rez age), `a` (auto-return proof) and `p` (transport calibration). Requests carry an intended-key count and an `END` sentinel; replies carry `checked`, `present`, the echoed mode and — for existence — **the missing keys themselves in full**.

Returning full keys rather than indexes is free, by a proof rather than an estimate: a K-key request is 37K+53 bytes on the wire and the worst possible reply is 37K−24, so **the answer can never be larger than the question**. That makes the unverifiable reply-side cap irrelevant to correctness. At K=50 both directions sit under 2048 bytes.

Six guards must all pass before a reply may refute anything: END sentinel (checked from the end, since empty tokens are dropped), count echo, **conservation** (`present + missing == checked == sent`, without which "all present" is an argument from pure absence), **canary anchors** (two independently-confirmed-live keys per batch, so a 100%-missing answer is trustworthy and a missing canary voids the reply), membership, and context (`sid` plus unchanged region handle — the bridge is an attachment and crosses regions with the avatar). Everything else is neutral. There is no path from a transport problem to a refutation.

Cadence: not before T+15s after handshake, so sweeps do not compete with the probe flood the ROC relies on for free confirmation; ordered escalated-first, then oldest-unconfirmed, then soonest-TTL. Bump `BRIDGE_VERSION` and the viewer-side minor version together; the existing mismatch-recreate dance pushes the update.

## Reconciliation principle

The live stream always wins; ROC pre-paints and gets out of the way. Mode A ghosts sit in mCacheMap under stable local_ids so stock probes/kills/stats flow through unchanged paths; compressed updates fold through cacheFullUpdate (dupe = confirm, CRC-change = confirm+dirty); uncompressed OUT_FULL rebinds by FullID. Probes racing ahead of async injection are harmless (stock total-miss path = cold-cache behavior; injection is idempotent). Kills: mode-A ghost ids are registered so KillObject reaches them (+1 refutation); mode-B ghosts are unregistered so id-reuse kills can't touch the wrong object. Aux reconciliation is inherent — every source (LayerData, handshake, ExtEnvironment GET) overwrites idempotently.

## Diagnosability: the "declined is indistinguishable from never ran" defect (fixed 2026-08-27)

For several sessions ghost injection appeared never to have run: every region logged `mode B, no injection`. It was false. The suffix at `ssrocghost.cpp:179` was printed from `rg.mModeA`, which was only ever *assigned* at the CacheID comparison — both earlier returns (no file, region-id mismatch) left it default-false, and the removal log was gated on `mPlanBuilt`, which was set unconditionally at the top of `armRegion`. So every first visit with no `.roc` printed a message claiming the simulator had restarted. `mMetrics.mRegionsModeB` — the only counter incremented on the real mode-B path — read **0** in the same session that logged three of those lines, and that same session shows `planned 4132 … injected 3154 … confirmed 3154, refutations 0, id-reuse 0`. Mode A worked perfectly. One log string manufactured a diagnosis that nearly bought an entire unnecessary subsystem.

The fix is a **closed reason-code set**, `ESSROCArmOutcome`, latched at every single exit from `armRegion` and reported once per region arrival at `LL_INFOS("SSROC")` whether or not anything was painted: `PENDING` (never the agent's region — the structural cap, and by far the commonest), `NO_HANDSHAKE`, `LOAD_IN_FLIGHT`, `NO_FILE`, `FILE_EMPTY`, `REGION_ID_MISMATCH`, `CACHEID_CHANGED`, `PLAN_EMPTY`, `INJECTED`. The `CACHEID_CHANGED` line prints **both** cache ids, because two different non-null ids (a restart) and a null on either side (a previous visit that never saw a handshake) are materially different situations that used to print identically. The session summary carries the whole histogram. **Rule for anything added here later: no reason may ever be inferred from a default value.**

Three structural caps, all verified, all larger than any injector change: only `gAgent.getRegion()` is ever armed (42 regions tracked, 1 armable); 39 of 42 regions were first visits with no file at all; and `promoted 0` across 232,167 records means the plan is the 180-minute recent window and nothing else. The exit histogram now reports the best distinct-day count any record in the region has reached against `SSROCPromoteDaysUnproven`, plus how many records are one day short, so "promoted 0" is readable as a countdown instead of a mystery.

## The arm-before-load race (fixed 2026-08-27)

`tick()` armed on the first frame where `mHaveHandshake && !mPlanBuilt`, with no check that the async disk read had landed — and `armRegion` set `mPlanBuilt` first and never retried. The handshake arrives on the `RegionHandshake` message; the read was kicked back at `LLWorld::addRegion`. On a teleport into a region with a multi-megabyte `.roc` and a busy worker pool the handshake wins, the region gets no ghosts for the whole visit, and it is then logged as "no file". `SSROCAuxMgr::RegionState` already had an `mLoadDone` and used it correctly; `SSROCGhostMgr::RegionGhosts` did not, so it could not tell "loaded, and it was empty" from "still in flight".

Arming now requires `mHaveHandshake && mLoadDone`, with a bounded wait (`SSROCGhostArmWaitSecs`, 8s) after which the region is armed anyway and latched as `LOAD_IN_FLIGHT` rather than being silently written off. If the file lands afterwards and nothing has yet been painted or refuted, `onRegionFileLoaded` clears `mPlanBuilt` and the tick re-arms for free.

## Local-id epochs: a live mode-A correctness hole (fixed 2026-08-27)

Found while evaluating mode B; it was live in mode A with no mode B built at all. The `.roc` stores **one** CacheID for a whole record set (`ssrocaux.cpp` stamps `file->mLastCacheID = mCacheID` whenever the handshake was seen, unconditionally), but the ledger only re-keys `mLastLocalID` for records the simulator actually mentioned. So after any visit whose CacheID changed, the file carries the **post**-restart cache id beside records still holding **pre**-restart local ids. On the visit after that, the CacheID comparison says mode A, the planner had no epoch check at all, and `injectOne`'s created path would invent a cache entry at the blob's own dead-epoch id — precisely "paint a remembered object where a different one now lives".

Closed with two per-record bits in the existing `mRecordFlags` word, so `SSROC_FORMAT_VERSION` stays at 6 and no user's cache is wiped: `SSROC_REC_ID_CURRENT` (the stored id came off the wire under the cache id this file was saved with) and `SSROC_REC_ID_STALE` (the cache id moved on while this record went unmentioned). **Neither bit set means a legacy file and is treated as usable**, which is what keeps existing caches working. `SSROCLedger::applyIDEpoch` runs once per visit, driven by whichever of the handshake and the disk read arrives second, and marks every record the simulator did **not** mention this visit; `confirmRecord` and `addRecord` set CURRENT and clear STALE, so a stale mark is a deferral that any fresh sighting rescues, never a death sentence. `ssROCBuildInjectPlan` refuses STALE records outright — not at a lower priority, because a stale id is not a weaker candidate, it is a different object. A stale root takes its children with it through the existing orphan rule.

## Closed questions (do not re-propose)

- **"Revalidate a stored local id by CRC before injecting it."** No. Measured on a real 4132-object region: only **404 distinct CRC values**, 15.5% of objects share the single value `9`, and adjacent local ids agree on CRC 22% of the time. A 32-bit field with that little entropy cannot distinguish "the same object" from "a different object at the same id", so a CRC test buys no safety and would read as though it had. This also explains why the CRC test at `llviewerregion.cpp:3167` is dead code on **both** ROC paths: the entry-exists path overwrites `crc` with `entry->getCRC()` before calling through, making the comparison tautological, and the created path has no pre-existing entry to compare against.
- **"The ROC never causes a send" as an audited invariant.** Replaced with an enforced one. `LLViewerRegion::addCacheMiss` now drops any id at or above `SSROC_SYNTH_ID_FLOOR` (`0xFF000001`), because `requestCacheMisses` turns that queue into a `RequestMultipleObjects` once a second and there are live paths that would feed it an id the ROC may own — `llvovolume.cpp` under `FSEnforceStrictObjectCheck`, and the parenting-cycle recovery in `llviewerobject.cpp`. The floor is chosen from measurement, not from the protocol: 556,931 real local ids across 43 SL regions sampled from both the `.slc` and `.roc` stores, peak `0x399791FA`, nothing at or above `0x40000000`. Nothing allocates in the reserved range today; the guard fires only on a grid whose id policy has changed, and warns once when it does.
- **Ghost selectability.** Owner decision 1 stands (selectable in mode A, because edit/right-click select auto-culls a ghost that is no longer there). It is now `SSROCGhostSelectable`, defaulted to today's behaviour, so a future mode B — where the id would be one the simulator never issued, making a grab nonsense on the wire and touch and sit silently fail — can flip it without an argument at the time.

## Mode B: measured before it is built

Mode B fires only when a region restarted between two visits, and it self-heals to mode A on the very next visit (`confirmRecord` re-keys from the simulator's own stream, and the exit path stamps the new cache id). Roughly one visit in seven for a daily region — and on that visit the plan is disproportionately empty anyway, because a region you are returning to after a restart is one you probably last saw more than three hours ago.

So it is **measured, not built**. On a `CACHEID_CHANGED` visit the ordinary plan is still computed and held as a FullID set (`SSROCGhostShadowMeasure`, default on); nothing is painted; the set is struck off as the simulator delivers each object unaided, and the count is logged at region exit against the total number of sightings. This is free precisely in mode B: a CacheID change makes `llvocache.cpp` discard the whole `.slc`, so the simulator must send a full blob for every object in the region and every one carries its FullID at blob offset zero. If the simulator turns out to deliver most of the plan on its own, mode B is not worth its risk; if it does not, that number is the justification for building it.

When it is built, the design is private-range synthetic local ids with FullID reconciliation, and **two things must ship together or not at all**: a FullID branch in `noteSimUpdate`, and an `obj->getLocalID() != ghost.mLocalID` adoption test in `retireGhost`. Without the second, every mode-B ghost stays LIVE, hits its TTL, and `retireGhost` kills an object that the stock re-key has already handed to the simulator — up to four thousand real, validated objects blinking out at once about ninety seconds after arrival, with no resend, because as far as the simulator is concerned the viewer has them. The purge must also invert (every id at or above the floor, unconditionally, including confirmed ghosts), `mByLocalID` must never be given a record's stored id as a second key, and `noteKill` needs a FullID path or the trash-the-region defence does not exist in the mode where a rebuilt region is likeliest.

## Owner decision points

1. Ghost selectability (mode A): selectable lets edit/right-click auto-cull dead ghosts (verified: plain left-click does NOT select, so the cull benefit is modest) vs unselectable click-dead furniture for ≤120s. Recommend selectable.
2. TTL behavior when bridge is absent: derez at TTL per spec (recommended; record survives) vs keep-rendering-until-contradicted (better visuals, longer stale exposure on demolished builds).
3. Trash-all threshold: max(10, 20%) conservative (recommended — resists false trash when you pick up your own prims) vs max(8, 10%) faster.
4. Scripted-object penalty: −0.10 lets CRC-stable vendors/orbs promote; raise to −0.15 or require bridge confirmation for scripted objects to trade coverage for safety.
5. Neighbor regions: v1 aux-only; given the fork's 2048m draw distance, neighbor ghosts may be wanted later (needs per-region budgets, accepts bridge-unverifiable ghosts).
6. IL_MODE_360 for ~30s post-TP to accelerate all-direction confirmation: extra sim traffic, nonstandard behavior, and it partially competes with the ghost win — measure first.
7. **Privacy — escalated, and it is now more than retention.** The ledger as designed persists third parties' `owner_id` and `group_id` per object, plus a [PARCELS] section holding other people's parcel owner/group/auto-return settings and prim counts, indefinitely (records die only on refutation) — harvested partly by silent probes the object owners cannot see. That is materially more than the `.slc` keeps today. Options: store salted hashes of owner/group instead of the UUIDs (equality comparison is all the immunity logic ever needs, so this costs nothing functionally), drop the [PARCELS] section between sessions, or accept as-is. **Recommend the hashes.** It must die on cache clear regardless — already wired.
8. **Griefing signature.** Stage C's bulk `ObjectSelect` plus Stage B's region-wide parcel enumeration run automatically and invisibly on every region entry. To an estate's anti-copybot heuristics that is the classic scraper profile — and it is *not* excused by "FSAreaSearch already does this", because FSAreaSearch only runs while its floater is open. Options: keep Stage C opt-in, restrict it to regions you visit repeatedly, or rely on `RocSweep a` where the bridge exists (which removes the traffic entirely). This is the one decision with real account-safety stakes.
9. Auto-return gate strictness: keep the hard gate (recommended — a penalty is fungible and can be bought back by unrelated score terms) versus promoting unproven objects immediately with a penalty.
10. `SSROCPersistenceDaysFull` (default 3) is the single knob controlling the bridge-free timeline: at 3, clean prims ghost on visit 3 and scripted on visit 4; at 6, scripted content never promotes without a bridge; at 2, everything is a visit earlier at the cost of promoting younger objects.
11. Shadow mode for absolute refutations: for the first 100 answered-missing results per grid, log the disagreement rate against an independent probe instead of evicting. Cheap insurance for the one mechanism that is allowed to delete data. **Recommend on, until the agreement rate is measured at ≥99%.**
12. Disk: 2GB roc/ watermark alongside the BC7 budget — confirm combined SSD footprint.
