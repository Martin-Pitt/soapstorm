# Atmo Magic: the ground strike - aura, amber, plasma, crawl, sparks, fire

Rationale for the 2026-09 rework of `sslightning.*`, `sslightningrender.*` and `ssLightning{V,F}.glsl`. The code carries one-line comments; the why lives here. Companion: `doc/archive/atmo_magic_lightning.md` (the original design, still right about scheduling, thunder, channel morphology and the compositing order), `doc/viewer/glow_and_alpha.md` (screen alpha is the bloom mask in this pass).

## The reference

A 25 fps dashcam clip of a bolt hitting a moving car on a wet road, frames 2-12 (t = 0 at frame 2):

| t | column | ground |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | wide blazing white core, pale blue-violet sheath, beaded; scene washed pale blue | over-exposed orange-white flare, 6-10 m wide, wider than tall |
| 0.16 | a beaded white-cyan plasma column ~2x the bolt's width, soft, upper part still white | a 15-20 m line of yellow-orange fire; the whole frame, sky and rain, washed orange |
| 0.24 | thinner, wisps and vortices; upper white, lower part cyan-green | a chain of discrete orange blobs along the road, reflected in the wet surface |
| 0.28-0.32 | curling wisps; the lowest knot greenish-yellow-white, several times brighter than the wisps | orange blobs, dimming |
| 0.36 | RESTRIKE: a chain of distinct beads down the same column, old wisps under it | fire still burning |
| 0.40 | faint grey wisps; the yellow knot still bright | dim orange, still there |

What that fixes in the model: the column's plasma phase lasts ~0.4 s and goes white-blue, white-cyan, grey while widening and breaking into curling wisps; only the foot goes cyan-green, yellow-green, then a yellow knot that outlives and outshines the wisps; the ground fire is amber, lateral (a line of blobs, not a spot), ignites over two or three frames and outlasts the column; a restrike can come 0.3-0.4 s later, down the same channel, as a beaded chain; the orange wash is scene-wide, so it is a light plus a veil, not a sprite.

## Two invariants kept

Strike geometry is decided at spawn, ten seconds early, from streams seeded by the fire time - the crawl, the spark table, the fire blobs, the aura discs, the per-node tip distances and pop thresholds are all built once. Per-frame work is closed-form evaluation of hashes and envelopes; nothing simulates state. The renderer stays stateless: every envelope it reads (`mChargeHeld`, `mHit`, `mFire`, `mPlasmaSince`, `mLastGap`, the per-stroke scale and drift) is computed in `SSLightning::advance()`.

## The vertex buffer (why immediate mode had to go)

`gGL` carries position, one texcoord and an 8-bit colour. Every ask below needs more per vertex: an amber weight that interpolates across a 100 m far-LOD segment, a plasma phase per node, an HDR brightness above white (8-bit colour clamps at 1.0, which is why the old `AMBER_BOOST` never showed), the strike point in a disc's own frame, an anchor depth. So the pass builds one `LLVertexBuffer` per frame (`MAP_VERTEX|NORMAL|TANGENT|TEXCOORD0|TEXCOORD1|COLOR`, U32 indices, the precip renderer's pattern) and draws it in one call; additive blending is order-independent so ribbons, sparks and discs share the buffer. The flash discs and the debug markers go through the same buffer, because a shader that declares `normal`/`tangent` makes the immediate-mode path read recycled memory for them.

Per vertex: `normal` = ribbon (strike seed, amber weight, plasma phase) / disc (anchor depth, height above surface in radii, hollow radius in radii); `tangent` = (HDR brightness, plasma strength or hollow, depth soft width, mode); `texcoord1` = ribbon (bead multiplier) / disc (strike point in uv units); colour = tint plus the bloom FRACTION in alpha. Modes: 0 live core, 1 sheath, 2 aura/flare/fire disc (the fraction of the mode carries the flare's share), 3 plain ribbon (sparks), 4 sky flash disc (the old cubic profile and alpha rule exactly), 5 flat fill (wash, markers), 6 occlusion box (writes zero, never discards).

Fragment output everywhere: `rgb = min(tint * bright * mask, 3)`, `alpha = glow * bloom_fraction * mask * min(bright, 1)`. Brightness above white reaches the tonemapper through the RGBA16F screen target; the bloom seed is never inflated by it (rule 3 of glow_and_alpha).

## The asks, one by one

**Sparks land and spread.** The old roll coupled vertical to horizontal speed (`speed x rise`), so most sparks flew 2-5 s against a 1.4 s life and never landed - and the strike retired at ~1 s anyway, cutting them off mid-air. Now each spark is a spawn-time seed: flight time 0.28-0.95 s (vertical launch derived from it, so every one lands), landing distance 3-28 m x (0.6 + 0.4 intensity) x `SparkSpread`, heading fanned along the crawl for 60 % of them and 40 % launched from crawl nodes, the surface crossing solved once (`surfaceZ`), a roof edge or wall (more than 2.5 m of drop or rise) letting the spark fly off. Colour walks white, orange, red; the head widens to a 1.2-pixel floor at range with its brightness handed back. Retirement now waits for the longest spark plus its secondaries.

**Charge aura.** Old: four discs re-hashed per frame from a spread that CONTRACTED as the charge rose (the haze grew, then shrank to half a metre), gated on at 20 % charge with a squared envelope and a cubed pulse (a pop), no depth fade (a hard chord through the road). New: five discs fixed at spawn (four patches on a 1.5-4 m ring plus the centre), radius (1.2 + intensity) x (0.7-1.3) m near, growing 30 % to 100 % over the first 60 % of the charge, arriving one by one, envelope `smoothstep(0, 0.35, held) * held^1.5` from exactly zero, pulse with a 0.45 floor. Held through the leader (`mChargeHeld`; `mCharge` itself snaps to zero at leader start, up to 1.2 s before contact) and decayed over 60 ms after contact under the flare.

Subtlety is calibrated by light, not by peak: `AURA_GAIN` 0.032 at dial 1 lands the time-integrated light at roughly a third of the old corona's (larger discs and a pulse floor against a tenth of the old peak); dial 3 is about the old amount of light, 10 the old peak. The dial goes to 10 for that reason.

Hollow: the literal ask was "fade out towards center". Per-disc hollows sum to the BRIGHTEST pixel at the attachment (four rings overlap there), so every disc dims toward the STRIKE POINT instead - each vertex carries the point in its disc's uv frame, the shader fades within a 2 m radius of it - and the flare fills it back in.

Clipping, three layers: (1) the pull - each disc is moved toward the camera along the live view ray by `min(clamp(0.12 d, 4, 120) * AuraPull, 0.5 d)` and its radius rescaled by the same factor, so it projects exactly where its true point does (parallax by construction) while its depth beats kerbs, cars and terrain lips; (2) a per-vertex height fade against the cached surface height, which is what removes the hard chord at grazing views where a metre of depth fade is a tenth of a pixel; (3) with a depth copy, an anchor compare (fade where the scene sits in front of ~55-70 % of the strike point's depth - lenient, because at a grazing view the road under the disc is nearer than the point without hiding it) and the puff-style soft fade against the disc's own drawn depth. The pull deliberately draws the disc over occluders within the pull distance - that is the "covers the location visually" ask.

**Amber gradient.** Old: computed per SEGMENT from the segment midpoint's height against a constant 18 m zone, applied flat to the whole quad - with 12-100 m segments at most one fell in the zone, a step on one quad, sub-pixel at a kilometre. New: `finishChannel` walks from the trunk foot up the parents accumulating path metres (`mTipDistM`), branches inherit their fork's figure, the crawl is zero; the renderer interpolates the weight per vertex. Zone = `AmberZone` (25 m) x (1 + 2 x ground distance / 1 km), capped at 2.5 km's worth, never under forty pixels, positive bolts 1.3x. Core tint mixes toward `AMBER_CORE` then `AMBER_HOT` in the lowest 12 %, sheath toward `AMBER_GLOW`, width flares 1 + 0.9 w, brightness 1 + 2.2 w^2 above white through the float attribute. Ground strikes pivot about the foot: the wind drift between strokes is weighted by tip distance so the attachment and crawl never move.

**Amber reuses the aura on hit.** Same five discs; each disc's colour is the SUM of the violet term and the amber term (`FLARE_MID x HitFlare x mHit x (0.5 patch / 1.0 centre)`), normalised so the tint stays 8-bit and the excess rides brightness, so the crossfade needs no palette switch and no pop. The flare's share `q` selects a power-law spike `1.6 q (1-r)^6` at the contact - the blazing point inside the wide glow of frame 2 - blending to the over-exposed `HOT_COLOR`. `mHit` sums fired strokes at 1.5x the stroke decay with a 20 ms square-root rise, so it fades with the bolt one decay behind it and every restrike re-lights it. The centre disc is a 1.6:0.8 ellipse (world-horizontal), radius 2 + 2 x intensity m.

**Plasma.** The dissolve's per-segment random pop is kept - the discard approach is right - but thresholds are now per NODE and spatially coherent (25 m value noise plus jitter), so the column breaks into stretches, and from its pop each stretch is drawn as a plasma quad with its OWN envelope: `0.5 x intensity x stroke scale x exp(-(since - 50 ms) / tau)`, tau 0.20 s in the air, 0.32 s at the amber foot. The old tail was multiplied by the stroke's 55 ms exponential and was at 3 % by the time the recorded column is still saturated. The quad widens 2x to 6x the core with age; the shader beads it, domain-warps it into curling wisps growing with age, erodes it with a rising threshold into stretches and knots, grains it at 30 Hz in ribbon space (not screen space), and walks the colour: air white, white-cyan, grey; foot white-yellow, yellow-green, yellow knot with an HDR boost where the eroded plasma is densest. Sub-pixel ribbons skip the warp and grain (`fwidth`), and a distance LOD fades the plasma strength as the core drops under three pixels. Plasma is drawn for the LATEST stroke only (plus the previous one when the gap exceeded 0.15 s, so grey wisps hang under a fresh bolt), never stacked per stroke. The live core beads too (`Bead`), and a late restrike multiplies the beading by how far the previous copy had cooled - the re-lit column is a chain of distinct beads. The cheap path (sheath, sparks, intact core with beading off) is one power and no hashes, so the dominant fill costs what it did.

**Ground crawl.** Grown in `buildChannel` right after the trunk, before any branches (the node cap cannot starve it), on its own stream: length 30 m x u^1.6 x `Crawl` (median ~9 m, a quarter under 1.5 m and skipped), lengthened by wetness at the foot, one arm or two (35 %, the second at 40 % length), 1.5-3 m steps, three pre-rolled headings per step scored by the wetness two steps ahead (`CrawlWet`), the surface from `SSLightning::surfaceZ` (surface field, then the flowmap's height capture, then terrain, never below water - no raycasts, which walk the whole octree), stopping at a 2 m jump. Crawl nodes join `mChannel` as non-trunk nodes off the foot so amber, plasma, joints and the leader all work unchanged; the renderer draws them as GROUND-CONFORMING quads lifted 12 cm (a camera-faced card at ground level buries its lower half in the road with a hard chord), with a thinner sheath and a faster plasma so a gap opens under the knot as in the late frames. The attachment search gains ten ring probes so standing water on open ground (where the flowmap has no columns) can pull the strike: a full puddle is worth three metres of height.

**Ground fire.** A third element, because the frames show the ground glow outliving the column two to three times and neither the aura (which must fade with the bolt, by request) nor the sparks can do that. Blobs seeded at spawn: one every ~1.8 m along the crawl (up to 14), a fan of five within 1.5-5 m when the crawl is under 4 m (so the impact-time fire line always exists), and landing embers under half the landing sparks (up to 16). Each ignites `dist / 60 m/s` after a stroke (the fire runs outward along the crawl), rises over 80 ms, and burns `exp(-age / (tau_f x life_mul))` with `tau_f = GroundFireLife / 2.5`, flickering, cooling from `FIRE_0` through `FIRE_1` to `FIRE_2`. Crawl and fan blobs age from the latest stroke (a restrike re-ignites them); embers from their own touchdown. Drawn as mode-2 discs with the same pull and fades as the aura, a small spike, a 2-pixel floor, within 800 m.

**Lighting.** The bolt light is unchanged. A second amber point light per ground strike (`FIRE_LIGHT`, radius 25 + 35 x intensity + crawl length, at the foot-to-crawl midpoint, driven by `max(mFire, 0.5 mHit)` and `FireLight`) is pushed after the bolt lights so it is the first to drop at the four-light budget - it is what turns the wet road orange and puts the reflections in the water. The scene-wide orange (frames 6-8 are orange in the sky and the rain, which a point light cannot do) is a fullscreen additive veil drawn in `renderFlash` before the puffs: `AMBER_GLOW x 0.12 x (0.85 mFire + 0.15 mHit) x intensity x FireLight / (1 + (d/80)^2)`, no bloom seed (a fullscreen alpha would dip the exposure), so at the stroke instant the blue flash discs dominate and the orange arrives ~0.1 s later.

**Restrike.** The fast series is unchanged (30-90 ms negative, 15-40 ms positive). After it, two unconditional rolls: with `LateRestrike` odds (0.45; positive bolts 0.65x) one more stroke fires 0.25-0.50 s after the last, at 0.75 scale. Rolling after the loop leaves every earlier draw where it was. Per-stroke wind drift is accumulated as `min(gap, 0.09)` per fast gap and 0.03 for the late gap - a dart leader follows the existing channel and must not land metres downwind of it.

**Retirement** waits for the longest of the plasma tail, the fire tail (`3.5 tau_f x 1.4`) and the last spark plus its secondaries, so nothing is cut off mid-air; the occlusion query name is released there and in `clear()`.

## Performance

The depth copy: `SSVolCloud::ensureSceneDepthCopy()` is the puff pass's copy hoisted out and stamped by frame and viewport, taken by whichever pass asks first. Post-deferred passes never write depth, so one copy per frame is exact for both. The lightning pass asks only with `SoftDepth > 0`, a ground show on screen within 400 m and not hidden - a clear sky pays the blit only for those seconds. It must be called before the pass binds its own program (the copy binds `gCopyDepthProgram` and rebinds the screen target). It binds under the reserved `depthMap` name, `ss_soft_on` gates every read (the flash pass runs before any copy exists), and `ss_clip` passes the projection's CONSTANT far plane. Observation, not changed here: `ssvolcloud.cpp` passes `camera->getFar()` (the draw distance) as the far plane for its own linearisation; the puffs' soft distance was tuned by eye against that, so it was left alone.

The occlusion query, kept minimal on request: one `GL_ANY_SAMPLES_PASSED` query per live ground strike within 1.5 km on the ground show's box (foot, crawl, spark landings, fire blobs), the box drawn through the pass's own program in a mode that can never discard, names from the octree's pool, the answer read the next frame without ever waiting, honoured only away from the contact and the restrikes (`mT < -0.3 || since the last stroke > 0.3`), and gating only the ground show (crawl, sparks, fire, aura, swarm) - never the column, the scene light, the flash or the sound. Early-Z already kills occluded fragments; the win is the CPU quad build. Skipped under shader profiling (which holds the one occlusion query GL allows) and below GL 3.3.

Per frame the joint-merge scratch lives on the renderer (no allocations), the joint pass runs only when a copy will draw, crawl nodes skip the puff transmittance walk (never in cloud), and the buffer is capped at 48000 quads.

## Determinism, honestly

Lengths, arms, steps, headings, thresholds, spark rolls and fire seeds come from streams seeded by the fire time, so every client agrees on them. The wet-steered heading choice, the crawl's and sparks' surface heights, the fire blobs' heights and the puddle probes read the LOCAL surface field and flowmap capture, so two viewers can differ at ground level by a few metres - the degree of divergence the attachment search already had (its flowmap columns are local too). Chosen over strictly shared geometry because puddles genuinely attracting the crawl was the ask.

## Dials

`GroundAmber` (strength, existing) and `AmberZone`; `Bead`; `Plasma` and `PlasmaWarp`; `LateRestrike`; `Aura`, `AuraHollow`, `AuraPull`, `SoftDepth`; `HitFlare`; `GroundFire`, `GroundFireLife`, `FireLight`; `Crawl`, `CrawlWet`; `SparkSpread`; `OcclusionCull`. All `SSAtmoLightning*`, on the Effects & LOD floater's Lightning tab with the existing rows. Tuning notes: `Glow` (0.4) is the bloom brake - the flare's spike adds up to 0.35 of bloom fraction on its own and the discs accumulate additively; if the scene dims after a near strike, lower `Glow` or `HitFlare`, not the colours.
